What Affects Website Cost?

Every business is unique — here are the key factors that shape your final price.

📄
Number of Pages

More pages mean more design and development time, which increases the overall cost.

🎨
Custom Design

Unique UI/UX designs tailored to your brand cost more than template-based websites.

🛒
Ecommerce Features

Payment gateways, cart, product management, and checkout add complexity and cost.

🔍
SEO Requirements

Advanced on-page SEO, keyword research, and structured data setup require extra effort.

📅
Booking System

Appointment booking, calendars, and scheduling integrations increase development time.

🌐
Hosting & Domain

Annual hosting plans and domain registration are additional costs to factor in.
